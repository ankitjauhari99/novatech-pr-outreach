Core PR plan

Objective: Increase awareness of NovaTech Pulse among technology audiences.

Target audience: Technology journalists, gadget reviewers, consumer-tech blogs and relevant creators.

Key message: A thoughtfully designed smartphone focused on useful everyday features and simple user experience.

Channels: Personalised email pitches, press release, product brief and review-unit coordination.

KPIs: Relevant contacts reached, replies, product-review interest, earned coverage and referral traffic.

Sample journalist pitch

Use this as a draft and personalise it before any real outreach.

Subject: Story idea: Introducing NovaTech Pulse — designed for everyday use Hi [Journalist Name], I’m reaching out on behalf of NovaTech, a fictional consumer-technology brand, to share a story idea about our concept smartphone, NovaTech Pulse. The product concept focuses on a clean user experience and practical everyday features. We thought it may be relevant to your coverage of consumer technology and smartphone trends. If this fits your editorial plans, I’d be happy to share a product brief and arrange a short conversation. No pressure if it isn’t a fit. Thank you for your time, [Your Name] PR Outreach | NovaTech [Your Contact Details]

Measurement and next steps

How the campaign would be evaluated.

Measurement plan

1. Track personalised pitches sent and replies received.

2. Record confirmed coverage with outlet, date and story link.

3. Review message relevance and journalist feedback.

4. Compare campaign results against the original objectives.

Ethical outreach checklist

✓ Research each outlet before pitching.

✓ Be accurate and transparent about product claims.

✓ Respect editorial independence and opt-out requests.

✓ Follow up politely; avoid spam and misleading promises.
